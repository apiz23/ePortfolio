# Product

## Register

brand

## Platform

web

## Users

Mixed audience: recruiters and hiring managers evaluating for full-time roles or internships, and potential freelance/contract clients evaluating for project work, plus fellow developers networking. They browse quickly on desktop and mobile and need to immediately understand who Hafizuddin is and what he can build.

## Product Purpose

A personal portfolio for Muhd Hafizuddin, a final-year Software Engineering student at UTHM Malaysia, built to win full-time roles/internships and freelance or contract project work at the same time. Success is a visitor believing his craft is strong enough to reach out — whether that's a recruiter emailing about a role or a client asking about a project.

## Positioning

A software engineer whose portfolio is itself proof of craft — built and animated with the same precision he'd bring to a client's product or a team's codebase.

## Conversion & proof

- Primary CTA: Contact (email / LinkedIn)
- Secondary CTA: View resume / download CV
- The line a visitor remembers after 10 seconds: this developer builds with real precision and production polish, not templates.
- Belief ladder: his work is real and shipped (projects) → he executes with a level of craft most portfolios don't bother with (motion, detail, pacing) → he's worth a reply, for a role or a project.
- Proof on hand: project case studies and resume already live on-site. LinkedIn recommendations/testimonials were flagged as available but not yet collected in-repo — pull specifics (quotes, names, links) before building a testimonials section.

## Brand Personality

Bold, kinetic, precise. Production value inspired by landonorris.com's craft — full-bleed sections, scroll-driven storytelling, hover-reveal detail — without borrowing motorsport iconography. This retires the previous "quiet confidence / technical blueprint" identity (Sheet 01 labels, redline crosshairs, drafting motif) in favor of a bolder direction; the concrete visual system (dark vs. light, palette, motion language) is still undecided and should come out of a `/impeccable shape` pass before implementation.

## Anti-references

- Generic AI-generated portfolio (gradient text, identical badge headers, glassmorphism cards, cookie-cutter section templates repeated across every section)
- Corporate/safe blandness — no personality, stock-photo energy, templated SaaS look
- Literal F1/motorsport branding (helmets, checkered flags, racing numbers) — the reference is landonorris.com's craft and motion discipline, not the sport's visual language

## Design Principles

1. **Motion is structural, not decorative** — scroll-driven storytelling and hover reveals carry meaning, paced deliberately per section rather than one uniform entrance applied everywhere
2. **Precision over noise** — every animation, spacing choice, and type size is deliberate; nothing reads as templated
3. **Dual-audience clarity** — every section reads equally well to a recruiter and a prospective client
4. **Proof-first** — projects and resume do the convincing; decoration never outranks substance
5. **Reduced motion is a first-class path** — every kinetic sequence ships a full crossfade/instant alternative, not an afterthought

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Full `prefers-reduced-motion` alternative required for every scroll-lock/kinetic sequence (crossfade or instant transition, never suppressed content). No explicit WCAG level requested beyond standard color-contrast and motion-safety practice.
